A SIX-DAY FAMILY VISIT

Big days. Excellent meals.

This recommendation has one hike only: Cañón del Nacapule in San Carlos. Downtown, golf, and every other activity remain above as flexible alternatives.

01

Arrive + gather

Early-afternoon arrival → settle in and catch up → backyard carne asada with family. Nothing else on the schedule.

02

Ride the dunes

Early drive toward the coast → guided sandboarding at Dunas de San Nicolás → Bahía de Kino beach walk and seafood.

03

Conquer San Carlos

Cañón del Nacapule hike—the trip’s only hike → swim or beach rest → seafood at a waterfront table → Tetakawi sunset views.

04

Hermosillo parks + lunch

Relaxed Parque Madero walk → lunch at nearby vegan Jaris or Calafate at Los Lagos → La Sauceda forest walk, workshop, market, or evening event.

05

Ures is unmissable

Tamales and traditional Sonoran dishes at Las Bugambilias → historic Ures stroll → regional sweets, crafts, and an easy drive home.

06

Brunch + goodbye

Pack at the house in the Bachoco area → early brunch at Casa Garmendia on Morelos → leave Hermosillo in the late morning.

DECEMBER FIELD NOTES

Bring the right little things.

  • ☀

    Use the daylight
    Outdoor mornings and golden-hour viewpoints make the most of shorter days.

  • ◐

    Layer up
    Desert days can feel mild while evenings, coastlines, and overlooks turn cool.

  • ⌁

    Check before driving
    Holiday hours, weather, road conditions, and beach services can change.

  • ♢

    Carry water anyway
    Winter is friendlier, but the desert still rewards hats, sunscreen, and hydration.

OTHER IDEAS / CHANGES TO CONSIDER

The current plan returns to Hermosillo each evening. One overnight could make a coast day slower, easier, and more memorable.
