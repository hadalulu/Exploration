A FLEXIBLE THREE-DAY PLAN

Good days don’t need to feel packed.

01

Meet the city

Slow breakfast → Museo Regional → Plaza Zaragoza → Cerro de la Campana at sunset → carne asada.

02

Find the sea

Early drive to Bahía de Kino → beach walk → seafood lunch in Kino Viejo → sunset on the road home.

03

Follow your energy

Bachoco hike for active crews, MUSAS for a gentler morning → coyotas and coffee → family cookout.

DECEMBER FIELD NOTES

Bring the right little things.

  • ☀

    Use the daylight
    Outdoor mornings and golden-hour viewpoints make the most of shorter days.

  • ◐

    Layer up
    Desert days can feel mild while evenings, coastlines, and overlooks turn cool.

  • ⌁

    Check before driving
    Holiday hours, weather, road conditions, and beach services can change.

  • ♢

    Carry water anyway
    Winter is friendlier, but the desert still rewards hats, sunscreen, and hydration.
