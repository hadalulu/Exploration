Six-day plan

The plan includes one hike: Cañón del Nacapule in San Carlos. Other activities remain available as alternatives.

01

Arrival and carne asada

Early-afternoon arrival → settle in → backyard carne asada with family.

02

Dunes and Bahía de Kino

Drive to Dunas de San Nicolás → guided sandboarding → Bahía de Kino beach walk and seafood.

03

San Carlos

Cañón del Nacapule hike → beach time → waterfront seafood → Tetakawi sunset.

04

Parque Madero, Jaris, and La Sauceda

Parque Madero walk → vegan lunch at Jaris → La Sauceda activity or evening event.

05

Las Bugambilias and Ures

Tamales and Sonoran dishes at Las Bugambilias → historic Ures walk → return to Hermosillo.

06

Casa Garmendia and departure

Pack at the house in Bachoco → early brunch at Casa Garmendia on Morelos → late-morning departure.
